English: Privacy Policy

Gigalite has no accounts or advertising, and it doesn’t track you.

Your photos and videos are analyzed and compressed only on this iPhone. Nothing is uploaded to us or to anyone else.

Purchases go through the App Store, and Apple handles the payment. We never see your payment details.

We use RevenueCat to keep subscription records and purchase statistics. It receives your purchase history and a random identifier created by the app, not your name or email.

You can remove all of the app’s data on this iPhone by deleting the app. Copies it saved to Photos stay in your library. To ask about or delete your purchase records, write to us.
